Methodology

Every report states exactly what was measured, how, and what wasn't. This page is the long version.

1. Money-query share of voice (50% of the score)

For each brand we define 8–10 money queries — the buyer questions that decide the category ("best X for Y", "X alternative", "X vs Y"). For each query we assess whether the brand is cited (named as an answer), mentioned (appears but not as a recommendation), or absent — and which brand wins the query instead.

Presence is assessed by sampling the retrieval layer: the top-ranked comparison articles, roundups, review pages and community threads that AI answer engines demonstrably draw on when composing answers, supplemented by direct engine sampling where access allows. Scoring: cited = 1, mentioned = 0.5, absent = 0, averaged across queries.

2. Citation-source footprint (30% of the score)

AI answers are assembled from a knowable set of sources. We assess brand presence in six channels: Reddit, Wikipedia, YouTube, review sites (G2, Capterra and category equivalents), comparison roundups, and the brand's own answerable content. Each channel is rated strong (1), weak (0.5) or absent (0), averaged.

3. Site AI-readiness (20% of the score)

A fully automated probe (open methodology, deterministic, re-runnable) checks:

  • llms.txt / llms-full.txt — the emerging standard for handing AI models a curated site map
  • robots.txt policy toward 14 AI crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, and others) — blocked, allowed, or unspecified
  • schema.org JSON-LD coverage — which structured-data types the homepage exposes
  • Extractable text volume — what an AI crawler reading raw HTML actually sees (JS-only pages often render as nearly empty)
  • Heading structure, meta description, FAQ signals, sitemap

Composite score

Visibility score = 50% answer presence + 30% citation footprint + 20% site readiness. Grades: 85+ A, 75+ B+, 65+ B, 55+ C+, 45+ C, below that D/F.

Beta disclosure. AI answers vary by engine, session, user history and model version — any single sample is a snapshot, not a census. During beta, engine presence leans on the citation/retrieval layer plus direct sampling where available, rather than paid API sweeps of every engine. We state this in every report because a visibility auditor that oversells its own certainty would be an irony too far.
