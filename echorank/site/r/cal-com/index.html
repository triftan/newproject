Executive summary

Cal.com has carved out total ownership of one AI-answer niche: any question containing 'open source' or 'self-hosted' scheduling returns Cal.com first, nearly every time. But the money is in the generic questions — 'best scheduling tool', 'best Calendly alternative' — and there, AI engines describe Cal.com through Calendly's frame ('an alternative to...') or omit it behind Acuity, Zoho Bookings and SavvyCal. The site is AI-readable (llms.txt present, 2,200+ extractable words) yet ships zero schema.org structured data, so engines get no machine-readable facts about pricing, product, or company to quote. The gap between niche dominance and generic invisibility is the whole opportunity.

Fix roadmap — highest leverage first

The five moves that most improve how often AI engines say “Cal.com”.

  1. Ship schema.org JSON-LD sitewideLow effort

    The probe found zero structured data. Organization, SoftwareApplication and Offer markup (with the unlimited-free-plan priced at $0) hands engines quotable facts — the cheapest fix with the widest reach.

  2. Publish own-domain comparison pages for every rivalMedium effort

    'Cal.com vs Calendly' answers are currently assembled from G2 and StackShare — turf that favors the incumbent. Honest, spec-level comparison pages on cal.com give engines a first-party source to cite.

  3. Target SMB-vertical queries where Cal.com is absentHigh effort

    Booking-for-salons/clinics/coaches answers name Acuity and Square, never Cal.com. Vertical landing pages + presence in vertical roundups opens a market currently at 0% share of voice.

  4. Seed the 'free scheduling app' claim everywhereMedium effort

    'Unlimited free events' is a category-best fact that engines under-report. Get it into the roundups, review-site listings and Reddit threads engines actually quote.

  5. Build the Wikipedia-grade citation baseHigh effort

    Engines lean on encyclopedic sources for company facts. Press coverage and a properly sourced article give answers a neutral anchor Calendly already enjoys.
