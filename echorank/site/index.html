What the audit measures

An AI search visibility audit answers one question — does AI name your brand at the moment of buying intent? — across three layers:

01

Money-query share of voice

The 8–10 buyer questions that decide your category, and whether AI answers cite you, mention you, or skip you — and who wins each one instead.

02

Citation-source footprint

AI answers are assembled from a known set of sources: Reddit, Wikipedia, YouTube, review sites, comparison articles. We measure your presence exactly there.

03

Site AI-readiness

An automated probe of your site: llms.txt, robots.txt policy toward AI crawlers, schema.org structured data, and whether your pages are extractable at all.
